Payment steps

  1. Voice payment

    An explicit tap starts listening. The phrase “pay twelve fifty” becomes ₹1250, with no wake word left on.

  2. Merchant process

    Two chai stalls 15 m apart need a ring to choose. A single nearby match goes straight to confirmation.

  3. Payment confirmation

    The lens shows the amount, the rupee equivalent and a countdown. One tap signs the intent; a second tap does nothing.
