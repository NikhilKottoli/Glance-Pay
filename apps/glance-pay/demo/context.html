The problem

Pay for chai without
taking out your phone.

Even Realities G2 smart glasses show text in the lens, have a mic and a tap/ring input — but no camera and no speaker. RupeeRail, the payment rail, expects a scanned UPI QR. Glance Pay bridges that gap.

What you say
"Pay twelve fifty"
What the phone finds
Location + seeded merchant registry → UPI QR payload
What the lens shows
Café X · ₹12.50 · tap to confirm

The flow

Voice → merchant → RupeeRail → rupees.

1. Start with a ring gesture and say an amount. 2. The phone ranks nearby merchants and, if it is ambiguous, asks you to pick. 3. The lens shows amount, merchant and a countdown. 4. One tap signs a fresh quote; RupeeRail pays the merchant over UPI. 5. The lens reports paid, pending or failed.

Safety by design: no auto-pay · single-use confirmation · capped session wallet · expired quotes are re-quoted · location stays on-device.

Two roles

User and Admin.

The user can connect a low-balance session wallet and see their past payments. The admin onboards merchants into the registry and drives the payment simulation for this demo.
